INT. REHEARSAL STUDIO — 11:30 PM

Single overhead tungsten spotlight. Sound of rain drumming against the tin shed.

KABIR (played by Bhavya, 21) stands facing the mirror. His script pages are crumpled in his fist. He doesn't look at his reflection; he stares through it.

KABIR
(to himself, adjusting his collar)
Every actor thinks they choose the role. Until the character starts making your choices for you.

He steps closer to the glass. Breath fogs the surface. With two fingers, he wipes it clear.

---page---

ANANYA (20) steps into the studio doorway with two cutting chais in a wire rack. She watches him from the shadow.

ANANYA
We've been rolling since 6 AM, Kabir. You need to drop the accent before you go home.

KABIR
Which home? The one in the script, or the one with pending rent?

She smiles, shaking her head. Hands him the glass.

ANANYA
Take 14 tomorrow morning. Make sure the director is satisfied with the actor.

CUT TO:

---page---

EXT. AUDITORIUM ROOFTOP — CONTINUOUS

City lights spread out below in an endless amber haze. Wind catches the corner of the clapperboard lying on a plastic stool.

KABIR looks out over the Bengaluru skyline. The silence between takes is where the story actually lives.

KABIR
When the red recording light turns on, everyone watches your face. Nobody looks at what your hands are hiding.

He opens his palm. The faded rehearsal token sits there.

---page---

The assistant director's voice echoes from down the stairwell: "Director Bhavya on floor in two minutes!"

Kabir takes one slow, deliberate breath. The exhaustion falls away like shedding a jacket.

KABIR
(smiling faintly)
Camera rolling. Sound speed. Action.

FADE TO BLACK.
