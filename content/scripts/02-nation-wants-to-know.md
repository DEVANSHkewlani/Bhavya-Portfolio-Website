INT. TV NEWS STUDIO — PRIME TIME

Six neon screens flashing breaking red graphics. Decibel meters vibrating in the red zone. Three panellists speaking simultaneously over each other.

ANCHOR (played with hyper-intensity by Bhavya) slams both palms onto the glass desk, knocking over a ceramic coffee mug that has no coffee.

ANCHOR
(bellowing into lapel mic)
Hold on! Do not speak! When I am speaking, you will not speak! The nation demands to know why this panel is not outraged enough!

---page---

PANELLIST 1 gestures wildly with a rolled-up magazine. PANELLIST 2 is muted but screaming silently in a mini picture-in-picture box.

PANELLIST 1
If you would allow me thirty seconds without thirty sound effects—!

ANCHOR
Thirty seconds?! In thirty seconds, history has already been broken, debated, and forgotten on digital reels!

Anchor turns directly to Camera 1. Eyebrows knit into dramatic fury.

ZOOM IN HARD:

---page---

INT. STUDIO CONTROL ROOM — SIMULTANEOUS

THE PRODUCTION MANAGER (also Bhavya, wearing headset and stopwatch) calmly sips juice while watching the chaos on twenty monitors.

PRODUCTION MANAGER
(into headset)
Cue dramatic sting four. Increase graphics font by twenty percent. Viewership just jumped twelve thousand.

Assistant tech presses three flashing red buttons. The studio floor shakes with sound effects.

---page---

Back on the studio floor, the Anchor holds a dramatic five-second pause. Total stillness. The music drops to a single menacing heartbeat.

ANCHOR
We will return after this commercial break. Don't touch that remote. The truth is scheduled for segment three.

Commercial graphic slams down with comic precision.

FADE OUT.
